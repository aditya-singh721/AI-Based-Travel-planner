## PRACTICAL TIPS  

1. **Weather** – Carry a light sweater for evenings (especially in Oct‑Nov).  
2. **Cash** – Most places accept cards, but keep ₹2,000‑₹3,000 in cash for small stalls and tips.  
3. **Transport in Delhi** – Use **Ola/Uber** or **Delhi Metro** (Yellow Line connects New Delhi railway station to most attractions).  
4. **Safety** – Keep your belongings close in crowded markets; avoid isolated streets after dark.  
5. **Connectivity** – Buy a **Jio/ Airtel prepaid** SIM at the airport (₹299 for 30 GB data).  
6. **Health** – Stay hydrated; carry bottled water and a small hand‑sanitizer.  
7. **Cultural etiquette** – Remove shoes before entering temples and mosques; cover shoulders and knees.
